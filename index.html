What we usually find on trucking websites

Most of these take minutes to fix, but they quietly cost drivers and loads every day until someone notices.

  • The website tells Google not to show it in search
  • Another company's phone number or name left over from a template
  • "Experience required" and "no experience needed" on the same page
  • No analytics, so nobody knows how many applications the site brings
  • The same text copied across several carrier websites
  • Domain or SSL about to expire with no one watching

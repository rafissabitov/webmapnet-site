Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

This policy explains what information WebMapNet ("we") collects when you visit webmapnet.com or contact us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Usage data. We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use the site: pages viewed, time on page, approximate location (city or region), device and browser type, and how you found the site. This data is collected with cookies and similar technologies and does not tell us who you are.
  • Information you send us. When you email us, for example to request a free site check, we receive your email address, your name if you include it, your website address and anything else you choose to write.

The site has no user accounts, no payment forms and does not collect sensitive personal information.

How we use information

  • To reply to your request and provide the services you ask for.
  • To understand which pages are useful and improve the site.
  • To count how many requests the site brings, without identifying you.

We do not sell or rent your personal information, and we do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Google Analytics and cookies

Google Analytics is provided by Google LLC. Google processes the usage data on our behalf; you can read how Google uses information from sites that use its services. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings, or opt out of Google Analytics with the Google Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works fully without cookies.

Hosting and email

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which may log technical data such as IP addresses for security. Our email runs on Google Workspace. These providers process data under their own privacy and security terms.

How long we keep information

Analytics data is kept according to the retention setting in Google Analytics. Emails are kept as long as needed to answer you and keep a record of our work together, then deleted on request.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us what personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Depending on your state, you may have additional rights under state privacy law. To make a request, email us at the address below. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Children

This site is for businesses and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the date at the top of this page.
